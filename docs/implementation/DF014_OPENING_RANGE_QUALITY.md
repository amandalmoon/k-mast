The unchanged DF013 engine retains half-open ranges `[09:00,09:05)` and `[09:00,09:15)`, official-session context, both channels' timely acknowledgements, exact initial opening observation, receipt/validation availability, a post-boundary observed watermark, the two-second guard and all quality locks. The collector records a potential finalized range only once, at its actual evaluation time, with source hashes. A subsequent quote must be received after that time, have a sufficiently fresh source time and be validated before use. Ask/bid availability is only a quoted possibility; no order, queue position, actual execution, impact or PnL is inferred.

The source/receipt difference bound stays 0–2 seconds, inactivity suspicion stays strictly over five seconds and quote freshness stays one second. Clock synchronization without an independently measured bound is UNKNOWN. Inactivity can reflect liquidity rather than packet loss; the conservative HOLD and the interpretation are separate. No thresholds are relaxed after observing data.

Raw trade status fields and ten-level quotes are retained. Independent calendar evidence is required to bind date-less quote clocks; receipt date is never silently used as business date. Static preflight paperwork is not proof of every live VI, halt, auction, resumption or correction state. Collector contexts therefore retain continuous-market/correction UNKNOWN until authoritative time-valid evidence exists, and these states block ORB/VWAP qualification. Merely receiving all four channels cannot clear that lock.

Opening/preopen auction observations are separated from continuous trading. Cumulative volume/value is not proof of receiving every trade. Eligible VWAP uses price times quantity from qualified prints; regressions, gaps, duplicates, ordering defects, zero/crossed quotes and stale/asynchronous observations are flagged. There is no interpolation or use of another venue's volume. Exact opening and availability requirements may cause HOLD even on a working connection; changing them requires a separate future protocol.

Public results explicitly list source/receipt distributions, validation delay, date defects, inactivity, cumulative inconsistencies, duplicate/inversion and UNKNOWN counts per symbol/channel where measurable. Currently every actual distribution is NULL. Seconds-only source clocks cannot yield millisecond network-delay accuracy. Future partial data must retain the original flags and failed-state reasons; it must not be promoted to complete exchange coverage.
