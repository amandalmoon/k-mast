# DF013 research direction decision

D1 counterfactual readiness is **D1_COUNTERFACTUAL_DATA_DEPENDENCY_PENDING**; B is **B_ORB_OFFLINE_INFRASTRUCTURE_VERIFIED**. Neither strategy is adopted, rejected on PnL, or allocated capital. Economic edge remains unverified and LIVE remains unapproved.

Existing D1 caches provide useful prior20 price windows, and two exact-day KRX snapshots have 24/21 overlapping ordinary-share price histories. However, all six lack a complete eligible corporate/PIT/availability/tradability/contamination chain. A title census and survivor panel cannot resolve that chain. The exact total scope and complete-unit operating cost are unknown. Starting broad D1 price downloads now would not directly answer the missing counterfactual question; defer that collection while retaining the hypothesis and immutable evidence.

The conditional next priority is a **small separately approved B observation pilot**, because its immediate falsifiable questions are whether PAPER actually delivers four acknowledged channels at open, whether eligible 5/15-minute ranges can be reconstructed without detected gaps, and whether fresh post-signal quotes are observable. This is a judgment about tractable information gathering, not a measured monetary information-value estimate or evidence that B will profit. Current access and full market-state/correction coverage are still unknown. A documented demo branch cannot settle them, and D1's dependencies do not imply B's success.

B investigates opening liquidity demand and persistent order imbalance, distinct from D1 issuer-disclosure timing. Depth snapshots are not a complete order-event feed: quote changes alone cannot identify all cancellations or the true causal order-flow process. Trade-side fields and queues require separate interpretation evidence before imbalance claims. A successful quality pilot would justify collecting clean multi-session RVOL histories and designing a preregistered lagged-quote execution/cost experiment; it would not validate an alpha or actual fills.

Reject or HOLD the next experiment if PAPER entitlement/quota is denied or unknown, official session/current security validity cannot be confirmed, streams omit the opening window, status/correction gaps remain material, quote latency/spreads defeat observability, or the hard budgets are exhausted. Do not rerank symbols after ORB outcomes, repair missing prints, infer normal state from silence, or expand to LIVE. After supply validation, independent economic evidence still requires preregistered hypotheses, real costs, risk/capital comparability, executable-price analysis, multi-regime/OOS data and central risk/OMS checks. Deployment and LIVE allocation require separate approval.
