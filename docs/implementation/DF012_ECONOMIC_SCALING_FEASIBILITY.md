# DF012 economic scaling feasibility

The measured census contains **620 D1 title candidates, 301 corporations and 393 receipt dates**, plus 130 potential D1 corrections. Cached original documents cover 62 distinct receipts, leaving 558 uncached originals. There are 24 cached correction ZIP metadata records and 22 parsed corrections, leaving 106 correction documents uncached, including prior unavailable responses. Document presence alone does not establish parent linkage, paid market acquisition, identity or tradability.

Eight events have historical identity mapping; six now have bounded technical replays (two old plus four new). None is a complete research unit with valid PIT non-event controls. Therefore **30 complete independent units are still required**, rather than 24. At least ten companies, twenty entry dates, five ex-ante bear-market events and 80% valid fixed-control coverage remain required. These minima do not assert that 30 correlated events are statistically independent.

The optimistic identity-only lower bound, conditional on recovering all eight existing cases, is 22 more identities and two more companies. A future separately preregistered paid-market-acquisition subtype could use at most seven of these eight because 352770 is noncash: its conditional bound would be 23 events and three companies. This is a feasibility annotation, not a change to the current frozen D1 definition. Actual corporations requiring investigation and population conversion rates remain NULL; the selected five-case 20% action HOLD frequency cannot be extrapolated to the census. Halt-specific population HOLD frequency is unknown.

The exact receipt-date and previous-calendar-date inventory across both markets has 1,238 distinct KRX date/market pairs. Forty-six exact snapshots are already cached; **1,192 remain**. `scaling-krx-date-inventory.json` enumerates them. This is a full-census planning inventory, not a 30-event operational proposal, trading-day calendar or authorization to query. The exact next subset, KRX request budget and additional authentication budget remain NULL until event/identity/control selection is frozen.

For 30 treatments and nominal three controls each, 120 windows require 240 RAW/ADJUSTED page identities **if** each fits one page, with no overlap/cache savings. This excludes preselection history, corporate-action evidence, corrections, retries, indices and authentication. Control selection first requires the eligible PIT universe's prior twenty sessions of prices, volume, turnover, trend and volatility at each decision date; downloading only the eventual 90 winners would not supply a valid selection panel. Universe size, history pages, industry evidence and actual complete request count remain NULL. Eighty percent coverage means at least 24 valid fixed triplets (72 control slots), not permission to select controls after returns are observed.

The unchanged DF011 contract fixes three same-market/same-entry-date controls using decision-time information. Future +5/+30 contamination auditing must report unavailable coverage and invalidate the fixed set where required, without future-informed rematching. The current census ends 2025-10-08, so absence of later records is not proof of no contamination. PIT/halt/action gaps and missing controls preclude causal inference.

Measured DF012 cost is 12 requests, including one authentication, with a 35.719851-second first-request-to-last-response window. Index reuse is 302/305 event-window slots, not independent observations. Complete eligible-unit acquisition cost, vendor cost, staff time, future conversion yield and full-unit request cost all remain NULL because the complete-unit denominator is zero. No assumed success yield is used to manufacture a budget.

Stop or retain dependency status if the fixed minimum complete units, companies, dates, bear contexts, matched coverage, action/PIT histories, executable assumptions or comparable net costs cannot be established. Do not relax thresholds, fill missing prices or rights, replace failed cases after PnL, or promote technical returns to event alpha. A concrete next request list requires a new frozen selection and explicit collection approval.
