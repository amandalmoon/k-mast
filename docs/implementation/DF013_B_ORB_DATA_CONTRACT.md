# DF013 B ORB observation contract

This is an offline observation layer, not a new strategy, order simulator or broker connector. The original v1.2 B candidate remains completed KRX opening-range 5/15-minute breakout, with separate VWAP/RVOL and execution research. No returns are calculated.

Each raw quotation frame has SHA-256, immutable storage, connection identifier, local receive sequence, actual timezone-aware receipt time and monotonic nanoseconds. Each row retains its source fields, row identity, channel, symbol and market/calendar context. Local sequence is not an exchange sequence. Identical payloads remain separate archived receipts; suspected duplicate rows taint derived metrics instead of silently increasing volume. Without a provider execution ID, identical legitimate trades cannot be distinguished perfectly.

Receipt is not parsing completion. The observation engine separately records validated availability, rejects a validation time before receipt, and filters every range/VWAP/quote evaluation by that availability. OBSERVED-origin input without measured validation completion is locked. Only SYNTHETIC fixtures may default to an explicitly assumed zero parse delay. Later validation or subscription acknowledgement cannot improve an earlier decision.

Exact upstream schemas are 47 fields for H0STCNT0 and 63 for H0STASP0. The preregistration's descriptive `STCK_SHRN_ISCD` symbol label maps explicitly to the actual `MKSC_SHRN_ISCD`; the protocol is preserved. Trades retain price, quantity, cumulative volume/value, BSOP_DATE and second-resolution trade clock, market/time/halt codes, VI reference price and other official fields. Quotes retain ten price/size levels on each side and source clock. Quote date is absent: without independently verified same-session calendar context, exchange datetime is NULL and the observation is locked. Receipt date is never silently presented as an observed quote business date.

Field presence does not prove full VI, halt, resumption, cancellation or continuous-auction state. Raw status codes remain preserved. Market context defaults UNKNOWN and requires independent evidence; the synthetic harness labels its context SYNTHETIC_ONLY. Auction and unknown-session observations are excluded from eligible continuous-session VWAP/ranges. An auction at 09:00 is not identified solely by its clock. Correction/cancel lineage defaults unverified, and cumulative volume/value regressions or missing increments taint the window.

Opening ranges use half-open exchange intervals `[regular_open, regular_open+5/15 minutes)`. Finalization requires receipt-time availability after the boundary plus a fixed two-second guard, a subsequent observed event watermark, first observation at the verified open, both channel acknowledgements received by evaluation time and no detected defects. A later acknowledgement cannot travel back into a prior evaluation; the quote diagnostic also requires subscription availability by signal time. The boundary print belongs to the next interval. Delayed data is unavailable before its actual receipt. No finalized high is exposed before completion. This only certifies the synthetic harness contract: seconds-only timestamps and absent exchange sequence cannot prove all real prints were delivered.

VWAP sums eligible trade price times quantity divided by eligible volume; provider cumulative values do not substitute for the continuous-session sample. RVOL remains `RVOL_NOT_READY` until twenty clean historical same-exchange/session/elapsed-time baselines exist; one live morning or provider prior-volume fields alone do not establish that history.

Diagnostics reject receipt/exchange order inversion, local sequence gaps/reuse, business-date mismatch, source-to-receipt lag outside 0–2 seconds, quote inversion/zero top liquidity, and observed inactivity over five seconds. Inactivity is a *missingness suspicion*, not proof of a missing print; it deliberately HOLDs sparse streams. Limits are conservative observation-test guards, not optimized alpha thresholds. Unknown status never becomes normal by default.

Signal availability, hypothetical decision, arrival and possible execution remain separate concepts. A later quote must be received strictly after the signal, have source time at or after the signal, be received by the evaluation time and be no more than one second old in both source and receipt time. It is a quoted possibility, not a fill: no queue position, hidden liquidity, actual impact or guaranteed quantity is inferred. Full spread/depth fields remain inspectable; no signal-price immediate fill is modeled.

Disconnects clear acknowledgements and permanently taint the affected opening window. A new connection ID never repairs missing history. The immutable archive checks raw hashes and receipt records across restart; duplicate receipt IDs, invalid frames, account/encrypted/control frames, provided secret bytes and storage overflow are rejected without printing inputs. Approval messages are not quotation data and are not archived here. There is no network collector entry point; an explicit PAPER/entitlement guard cannot initiate a connection.

Synthetic evidence covers 1,802 messages with range highs 100/200 and arithmetic VWAP, plus fault-injection tests. None is empirical market evidence. Actual latency, throughput, reliable coverage, provider cost and operating cost remain NULL.
